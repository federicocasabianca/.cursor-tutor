## Summary
A comprehensive strategy to reverse declining GMV per session and AOV trends in the DACH desktop market while maintaining strong funnel conversion rates and leveraging the platform's growing user base and improved retention metrics.

## Core Challenge
The desktop DACH market is experiencing declining value per transaction (GMV per session down 36.7%, AOV down 10.0% from Aug 2024 peaks) despite stable funnel performance and growing overall GMV. This suggests a buyer experience degradation that's not captured by traditional conversion metrics, requiring a multi-faceted approach to restore per-session value.

## Goals
1. **Restore GMV per session** to Aug 2024 levels (0.6694) within 6 months
2. **Improve AOV** by 15% from current Jul 2025 baseline (7.7481) within 6 months
3. **Maintain or improve** current funnel conversion rates (Search→Material: 0.7941, Material→Cart: 0.0975)
4. **Preserve** the positive retention trend (currently 0.3145)
5. **Increase** high-value material conversion rates by 25% within 6 months

## Pillars
1. **Value Proposition Enhancement**
   - Optimize material pricing strategies for high-value content
   - Develop premium material bundles and packages
   - Implement dynamic pricing based on user segments and material value

2. **Personalization & Targeting**
   - Surface high-value materials to high-intent users
   - Implement user segmentation based on historical AOV and purchase patterns
   - Develop recommendation algorithms that prioritize value over volume

3. **User Experience Optimization**
   - Streamline the purchase flow for high-value materials
   - Implement social proof and scarcity indicators for premium content
   - Enhance material preview and discovery features

4. **Market Intelligence & Competitive Analysis**
   - Monitor DACH market pricing trends and competitive positioning
   - Analyze seasonal patterns to identify cyclical vs. structural declines
   - Conduct user research to understand changing buyer preferences

## Initiatives
1. **Immediate (0-30 days)**
   - Launch A/B testing for pricing strategies on high-value materials
   - Implement user segmentation analysis to identify high-value buyer cohorts
   - Begin competitive pricing analysis in the DACH market

2. **Short-term (1-3 months)**
   - Develop and test premium material bundles
   - Implement personalization algorithms for material recommendations
   - Launch user research to understand buyer behavior changes

3. **Medium-term (3-6 months)**
   - Scale successful pricing and bundling strategies
   - Implement advanced personalization features
   - Launch premium material discovery features

4. **Long-term (6+ months)**
   - Evaluate and iterate on strategy effectiveness
   - Expand successful initiatives to other markets
   - Develop predictive models for AOV optimization

## Risks & Assumptions
**Risks:**
- Aggressive pricing changes could negatively impact conversion rates
- Personalization changes might reduce overall material discovery
- Market conditions may continue to deteriorate regardless of platform changes
- Seasonal factors might mask the effectiveness of interventions

**Assumptions:**
- The decline is primarily platform-related rather than market-wide
- Users will respond positively to improved value propositions
- Funnel performance improvements can be maintained while optimizing for value
- The growing user base provides opportunities for A/B testing and optimization

## Evidence
- **Declining metrics**: GMV per session (0.6694 → 0.4236), AOV (8.6090 → 7.7481)
- **Stable performance**: Funnel conversion rates maintained or improved
- **Growth indicators**: Overall GMV increased from €1.2M to €1.5M
- **User engagement**: Retention rate improved from 0.2974 to 0.3145
- **Market context**: DACH desktop market showing volume growth but value decline
