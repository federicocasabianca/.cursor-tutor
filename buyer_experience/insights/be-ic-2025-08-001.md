### Rationale
The desktop DACH market shows a concerning trend where GMV per session and AOV have declined from their August 2024 peaks, suggesting buyers are spending less per session despite maintaining engagement through the conversion funnel. This indicates a potential degradation in buyer experience or value proposition that's not captured by traditional funnel metrics.

### Evidence summary
- **GMV per session**: Declined from 0.6694 (Aug 2024) to 0.4236 (Jul 2025), a 36.7% decrease
- **AOV**: Decreased from 8.6090 (Aug 2024) to 7.7481 (Jul 2025), a 10.0% decrease
- **Funnel stability**: Search→Material CR improved from 0.7667 to 0.7941, Material→Cart CR improved from 0.0846 to 0.0975
- **Retention**: Slightly improved from 0.2974 to 0.3145
- **GMV**: Increased from €1,219,864.92 to €1,549,537.72, suggesting volume growth offsetting per-session decline

### Counter-evidence
- Overall GMV has increased year-over-year, indicating the platform is still growing
- Funnel conversion rates have improved, suggesting the user journey is becoming more effective
- Customer retention rate has improved slightly
- The decline may be seasonal or cyclical rather than structural

### Implications
This trend suggests that while the platform is successfully converting more users through the funnel, the value per transaction is declining. This could indicate:
1. **Price sensitivity**: Buyers may be more price-conscious or finding lower-cost alternatives
2. **Product mix changes**: Shift toward lower-value materials
3. **User behavior changes**: More casual browsing vs. high-value purchases
4. **Market dynamics**: Increased competition or changing buyer preferences

### Experiments
1. **A/B test pricing strategies** for high-value materials to understand price sensitivity
2. **Personalization tests** to surface higher-value materials to high-intent users
3. **Bundle testing** to increase AOV through strategic material combinations
4. **User segmentation analysis** to identify which buyer segments are driving the decline

### Metrics to watch
- **GMV per session** (monthly trend)
- **AOV** (monthly trend)
- **High-value material conversion rates**
- **User segment performance** (new vs. returning, high-value vs. low-value buyers)
- **Seasonal patterns** to determine if decline is cyclical
- **Competitive pricing analysis** in the DACH market
