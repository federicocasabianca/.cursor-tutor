## Purpose (our “why?”)
**Our purpose is to improve education.**
Education is the future! And yet today’s schools could be so much better, for teachers and students alike.

## Vision (our “what” do we want to achieve?)
**Our vision is that every teacher is perfectly prepared for lasting impact.**
Imagine a world where every teacher feels perfectly prepared for their lessons. They would feel fully empowered to teach with confidence and excellence. Preparation is more than readiness; it’s about equipping teachers with the right resources and tools to make a profound difference in their students’ lives. At eduki, we are driven by this vision and work towards making it a reality for every teacher. 

## Mission (our “how” do we get there? What’s our   approach?)
**Our mission is to provide teachers with the resources and tools to deliver the impactful education.**
Teachers are at the central actors of every classroom, shaping the future of their students. Yet they are often left alone carrying that responsibility. We help them by providing the tools and resources they need. So that they can deliver the most impactful education.
