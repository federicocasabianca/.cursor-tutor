# Insight Rubric (v2 with core gates + scored quality)

## Core Criteria (must be present, else **Not Ready**)
- **Clear Claim** — single, falsifiable, specific.
- **Evidence-backed** — references ≥2 quality signals (and their windows/samples).
- **Business Relevance** — ties to OKRs/goals (list them).
- **Root Cause Analysis** — includes primary cause and a short 5-whys trail.

If any core criterion is missing → mark **Not Ready** and list what to add.

## Quality Indicators (score 0–2 each; sum shown)
1) **Depth** — moves beyond symptoms; “why” analysis reveals motivations.
2) **Novelty** — challenges assumptions or status quo (use `novelty_factor`).
3) **Customer Impact** — quantifies pain severity & affected segments/size.
4) **Actionability** — prioritized actions (immediate/short-term/long-term) tied to metrics.
5) **Measurability** — baseline/target/timeframe or explicit measurement plan.
6) **Clarity** — concise language, no jargon; easy to brief stakeholders.
7) **Evidence Quality** — signals are recent/relevant; limitations called out.
8) **Strategic Priority** — stated `strategic_priority` is justified.

**Assessment Bands**
- **Weak**: Meets core criteria but scores ≤8/16 or thin depth/novelty.
- **Medium**: 9–12/16 with solid relevance and some novelty.
- **Strong**: ≥13/16 with high business impact, novelty, and clear metrics.
