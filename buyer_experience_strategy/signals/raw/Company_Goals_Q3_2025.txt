4. Leverage word of mouth through better referral program
We’re facing early signs that our current growth trajectory may be reaching its limits—something we’ve never encountered before at eduki. To return to a sustainable path of user growth, we must activate every possible lever. A stronger, more effective referral program is one of the most promising opportunities.
Why focus on referrals? Surveys consistently show that word of mouth is a key driver of growth for us. While we already have a $3 incentive-based referral program in place, it raises two critical questions:
- Does it truly motivate our users to recommend eduki to others?
- Are we reaching and converting the right users—especially paying buyers in the ES market?
This project aims to reimagine our referral approach to unlock organic growth, with a specific focus on quality and resonance in the ES world.
KR1: Gain a clear understanding of the current referral program's performance and impact
KR2: Design a new, high-impact referral program based on behavioral insights, research, and creative exploration tailored to the ES (plus 1-2 promising LATAM markets)
