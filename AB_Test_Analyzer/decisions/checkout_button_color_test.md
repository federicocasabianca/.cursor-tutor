# Decision: Checkout Button Color

**Test name:** checkout_button_color_test  
**Area:** Checkout  
**Decision:** Ship (treatment wins)

## Primary metric + outcome
- **Checkout conversion rate:** +0.4 pp (7.8% → 8.2%), in line with MDE. Significant positive movement.

## Why
Treatment (yellow button) beat control on the primary metric. Guardrails were stable (bounce and error rate unchanged or slightly better). No concerning movement on secondary metrics. Roll out the yellow CTA.

## What we learned
- Button salience at the final step can move checkout conversion at our baseline.
- Small color change, measurable lift—useful signal for future CTA tests on checkout.

## Next steps
- Roll out yellow button (e.g. 100% or gradual by segment).
- Document for future checkout/CTA experiments.
