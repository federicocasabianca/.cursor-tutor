## Opportunities (Feature Development Suggestions)

1. **Restore and enhance price / “kostenlos” filters in the app**
   - Several users (e.g. 341202, 512764, 2608095, 2313960, 3782667) report that the **“kostenlos”/free** filter or price‑ascending sort is missing or unusable, making it hard to discover free or low‑priced content and leading to frustration.

2. **Expose seller metrics and cockpit inside the app**
   - Sellers (e.g. 2289230, 2787183, 4390029) struggle to see **sales, earnings, and performance** from the app and request a proper **Verkäufer‑Cockpit** similar to the web.

3. **Improve discoverability and organization of purchased materials**
   - Multiple users (e.g. 2788415, 3867978, 223047) find the app **confusing for locating purchased content**, bundles, or archived materials and ask for clearer navigation and better structuring (e.g. folders, archive, “already purchased” indicators that work reliably).

4. **Clarify and expand payment options and promotions**
   - Some users (e.g. 86240, 4419945, 4515922, 4338827) request clearer support for **invoice purchase, vouchers, and discounts**, and expect promised promotional credits to be easy to redeem.

---

## Bugs (Issues to Fix)

### Critical Bugs

1. **Paid materials not downloadable or missing from “My Materials”**
   - Users from multiple countries (e.g. 3718826, 3926389, 4007568, 4094243, 3386056, 3203547) report that **paid materials never arrive or don’t appear** under their materials, despite successful charges.

2. **App instability and crashes blocking access to content**
   - Some reports (e.g. 83855, 647162, 377393) show **App Crash** feedback tied to not being able to open or access downloaded materials at all.

3. **Search and filtering reliability issues**
   - Users (e.g. 2485288, 2840695, 34502) describe **search not returning expected results** or filters behaving inconsistently, which directly impacts their ability to find relevant materials.

### Other Bugs

4. **Invoices and receipts not accessible in the app**
   - Users (e.g. 323884, 1416194, 4234211) cannot find or download **invoices / annual overviews** from the app, especially for tax purposes.

5. **Language / localization issues**
   - Feedback includes **language switching problems** (e.g. app switching to German unexpectedly, missing Turkish or Arabic support), making the experience confusing for some non‑DE users.
